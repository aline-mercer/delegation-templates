# Away note for running work

Written for: whoever, or whatever, is covering while you cannot answer questions.
Use it when: you will be away, off sick or moving to other work, and jobs are still in motion.

> Guidance lines start with `>`. Delete them when you copy this.
> Most people write the inbox message. This is the other one: what happens to the work that keeps moving.
