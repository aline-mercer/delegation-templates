# Decision log

Written for: you, later.
Use it when: you or a helper make a choice you may need to explain, change or repeat. That includes the choice to hand a task over or keep it.

> Guidance lines start with `>`. Delete them when you copy this.
> Write each entry on the day of the decision. Reasons are clearest then and fade within days. Put the newest entries on top.

## Entry template

Copy this block for each decision.

### Decision ____: ____ (short title)

- Date: ____
- The decision, in one sentence: ____
- The reason, in a clause or two. What problem did it solve? ____
- What we rejected, and why:
  - ____ because ____
  - ____ because ____
- Decided by: ____
- Look at it again: ____ (a date, or a trigger such as "if the volume doubles")
- Status: [ ] active  [ ] superseded by decision ____
- Related: ____ (a brief, a job card or a file)

## Delegate or keep?

> A short check before you hand something over. Fill in four numbers.

- Task: ____
- Minutes to write a good brief: ____
- Minutes to review the result: ____
- Minutes to do it myself: ____
- Will I do this again: [ ] yes, often  [ ] now and then  [ ] once

If brief plus review is clearly more than doing it yourself, and you will not repeat the task, do it yourself. If you will repeat it, the brief is an investment: keep it as a template.

Also ask whether the task needs your judgment at every step, or only a check at the end. Judgment at every step is a sign to keep it.
